- To prove two distinct normalized factors are equal or coprime, use `UniqueFactorizationMonoid.normalizedFactors_eq_of_dvd (a : α) : ∀ᵉ (p ∈ normalizedFactors a) (q ∈ normalizedFactors a), p ∣ q → p = q` — called as `normalizedFactors_eq_of_dvd f p hp' q hq' hdvd` after stripping `toFinset` with `Multiset.mem_toFinset.mp`; paired with `Irreducible.coprime_iff_not_dvd`, this closes pairwise-coprime goals without going through `Associated` + `normalize_eq`.
- To prove monic `f = ∏ p ∈ (normalizedFactors f).toFinset, p ^ (normalizedFactors f).count p`, rewrite the LHS via `conv_lhs => rw [← hf.normalize_eq_self, ← UniqueFactorizationMonoid.prod_normalizedFactors_eq hf0]` then `exact Finset.prod_multiset_count _` — `Monic.normalize_eq_self` gives `f = normalize f`, `prod_normalizedFactors_eq` gives `normalize f = (normalizedFactors f).prod`, and `Finset.prod_multiset_count` regroups a multiset product as `∏ m ∈ toFinset, m ^ count m`.
- For the abstract `p ∣ r → ker(aeval T p) ≤ ker(aeval T r)` goal, the direct tactic sequence is `obtain ⟨q, hq⟩ := h; rw [hq, mul_comm, map_mul, Module.End.mul_apply, hv, map_zero]` — `mul_comm` rewrites `p*q` to `q*p` so composition order puts `aeval T p` last, and `Module.End.mul_apply` unfolds `(f * g) v = f (g v)` in the endomorphism ring (not `LinearMap.mul_apply`, which doesn't exist).
- `UniqueFactorizationMonoid.normalizedFactors (f : Polynomial K)` over a field does NOT elaborate under bare `[Field K]` — it needs `NormalizationMonoid (Polynomial K)`, which derives from `DecidableEq K` (via `CommGroupWithZero K`'s `NormalizedGCDMonoid` instance), so use `classical` in the parent proof and add `[DecidableEq K]` to every sub-goal mentioning `normalizedFactors`/`.toFinset`/`.count`; per-member irreducible+monic facts come from `Polynomial.mem_normalizedFactors_iff (hq : q ≠ 0) : p ∈ normalizedFactors q ↔ Irreducible p ∧ p.Monic ∧ p ∣ q` (in `Mathlib.Algebra.Polynomial.FieldDivision`).
- The hard `ker(aeval T (∏ i, q i)) ≤ ⨆ i, ker(aeval T (q i))` direction needs NO Finset.erase machinery — `induction n` (auto-generalizes `q`/`hcop`, no revert) then `rw [Fin.prod_univ_succ]`, get `IsCoprime (q 0) (∏ i, q i.succ)` via `IsCoprime.prod_right` + `hcop (Fin.succ_ne_zero i).symm`, split with `rw [← Polynomial.sup_ker_aeval_eq_ker_aeval_mul_of_coprime T hco]` (namespace is `Polynomial`, NOT `LinearMap` as the strategist directive claims), then `sup_le` with `le_iSup ... 0` and the IH on `fun i => q i.succ`.
- To prove `IsCoprime (q i) (∏ j ∈ Finset.univ.erase i, q j)` from pairwise coprimality, `rw [IsCoprime.prod_right_iff]; intro j hj; exact hcop ((Finset.mem_erase.mp hj).1).symm` — `IsCoprime.prod_right_iff` reduces to per-factor, and `Finset.mem_erase.mp` extracts `j ≠ i` from membership in the erased set.
- To prove `ker(aeval T (q j)) ≤ ker(aeval T (∏_{k∈s} q k))` from `j ∈ s` (divisibility-based kernel inclusion, no coprimality), use `obtain ⟨r, hr⟩ := Finset.dvd_prod_of_mem q hmem; rw [hr]; exact le_sup_left.trans Polynomial.sup_ker_aeval_le_ker_aeval_mul` — there is no `ker_aeval_le_of_dvd` in Mathlib; the real idiom factors via `q j * r` and applies `Polynomial.sup_ker_aeval_le_ker_aeval_mul`.
- To prove `iSupIndep (fun i => ker (aeval T (q i)))` for pairwise-coprime `q`, `rw [iSupIndep_def]; intro i` then close per-`i` with `(Polynomial.disjoint_ker_aeval_of_isCoprime T h_cop).mono_right h_le`, where `h_le : (⨆ (j) (_ : j ≠ i), ker (aeval T (q j))) ≤ ker (aeval T (∏ j ∈ Finset.univ.erase i, q j))` and `h_cop : IsCoprime (q i) (∏ j ∈ univ.erase i, q j)`; this turns n-fold independence into two single-`i` facts.
- The `Finset.equivFin`-based reindexing of a Finset product `∏ p ∈ s, F p` to `∏ i : Fin s.card, F ↑(s.equivFin.symm i)` closes with `rw [← Finset.prod_attach s F]; exact (Equiv.prod_comp s.equivFin.symm (fun x => F ↑x)).symm`; per-element predicates transfer via `(s.equivFin.symm i).property` and injectivity via `g.injective (Subtype.coe_injective h)`.
- For `∃ (n : ℕ) (p : Fin n → ...) ...` factorization goals, split into a Finset-indexed existence sub-goal (carrying the UFD/`normalizedFactors` math) plus a generic `Finset.equivFin`-based reindexing lemma that lifts all per-element predicates to `Fin n`; keeps the math free of `Fin` bookkeeping.
