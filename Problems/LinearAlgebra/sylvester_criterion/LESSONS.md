- `(M.submatrix f f).toBlocks₁₁.IsHermitian` closes in two lines: `simp only [Matrix.toBlocks₁₁]` (unfolds to `submatrix Sum.inl Sum.inl`), then `exact hHerm.submatrix (↑f ∘ Sum.inl)` — `Matrix.IsHermitian.submatrix` accepts composed embeddings directly; do NOT attempt `funext` + `conjTranspose` unfolding.
- To prove `(S : Matrix (Fin 1) (Fin 1) ℝ).PosSemidef` from `0 < S.det`: rewrite `S = Matrix.diagonal (fun _ => S 0 0)` (closes by `ext i j; fin_cases i; fin_cases j; simp [Matrix.diagonal]`), then `rw [Matrix.posSemidef_diagonal_iff]` and supply `le_of_lt (Matrix.det_fin_one S ▸ h)` — no explicit `IsHermitian` step needed.
- A leading principal minor of the top-left block `(M.submatrix finSumFinEquiv finSumFinEquiv).toBlocks₁₁` at `k : Fin n` equals `leadingPrincipalMinor M k.castSucc` (the block is `M.submatrix (Fin.castAdd 1) (Fin.castAdd 1)`, val-preserving), and the equality closes with `simp only [leadingPrincipalMinor, Matrix.toBlocks₁₁]; congr 1` — no manual `ext`/`Fin.ext` needed; then positivity is just `hMinors k.castSucc`.
- The Schur complement in the `finSumFinEquiv (m:=n) (n:=1)` block split is a `Matrix (Fin 1) (Fin 1) ℝ`, so prove its `PosSemidef` via determinant (NOT via `PosSemidef.fromBlocks₁₁`, which needs `M.PosSemidef` and is circular in the induction step): `det M = det A · det S` (`det_fromBlocks₁₁`, `Invertible A` from `hApd.isUnit.invertible`) with `det M > 0`, `det A > 0` gives `0 < S.det`, and a `Fin 1` matrix with positive det is PosSemidef (Hermitian automatic for 1×1 real).
- Reduce `M.PosSemidef` (size n+1) to a Schur complement via: `rw [← Matrix.posSemidef_submatrix_equiv (finSumFinEquiv (m:=n) (n:=1))]` then `rw [← Matrix.fromBlocks_toBlocks _, hC, Matrix.PosDef.fromBlocks₁₁ _ _ hApd]` where `hApd` is the top-block PosDef and `hC : toBlocks₂₁ = toBlocks₁₂.conjTranspose`; the lemma is `Matrix.PosDef.fromBlocks₁₁` (NOT `Matrix.PosSemidef.fromBlocks₁₁`), get its `[Invertible A]` instance from `hApd.isUnit.invertible`, and write conjTranspose as `.conjTranspose` (the `ᴴ` glyph parse-fails).
- `leadingPrincipalMinor M (Fin.last n)` is **definitionally** equal to `M.det` (the `castLE` at `Fin.last n` is the identity on `Fin (n+1)`, so `M.submatrix id id = M` by rfl), meaning `exact hMinors (Fin.last n)` closes `0 < M.det` directly — no `simp`, `rw`, or explicit equality proof needed.
- Citing a proved sibling (e.g. `posdef_of_possemidef_det_ne_zero`) directly in a strategy patch fails `lake build` with `Unknown identifier` — sibling lemmas from other strategy modules are NOT auto-imported; re-declare the lemma as your own `new_<slug>.lean` sub-goal (Tier-1 dedup aliases it to the existing proof) and reference that instead.
- In the Schur factorization `M.det = toBlocks₁₁.det * (toBlocks₂₂ - X * toBlocks₁₁⁻¹ * toBlocks₁₂).det`, `Matrix.det_fromBlocks₁₁` yields `X = toBlocks₂₁` (lower-left block), NOT `toBlocks₁₂.conjTranspose` — the conjTranspose form is equal only under `M.IsHermitian`, so any goal stating it must carry `hHerm` or it is FALSE (counterexample `!![1,1;2,0]`: det = -2 but conjTranspose-RHS = -1); convert via sibling `block_conjtranspose hHerm`.
- Dimension-induction on `Matrix (Fin n)` goals: `revert M; induction n with | zero | succ n ih` gives `ih : ∀ M : Matrix (Fin n) .., M.IsHermitian → .. → M.PosDef`, exactly the shape to feed an `(n+1)` step lemma — extract that step as a Backward sub-goal taking `ih` as an explicit hypothesis; base case is the vacuous `Fin 0` matrix.
- PosSemidef+det≠0 ⇒ PosDef closes directly: `rw [hM.isHermitian.posDef_iff_eigenvalues_pos]; intro i; refine lt_of_le_of_ne (hM.eigenvalues_nonneg i) ..` with non-vanishing from `hM.isHermitian.det_eq_prod_eigenvalues` + `Finset.prod_eq_zero` (no Schur-complement needed for this upgrade brick).
