## Strategic notes (from Manifest.md)
Classical content (Humphreys §7.2): every cyclic highest-weight sl₂-module
of integer highest weight `n` is irreducible. The (n+1)-dimensional span
of `{v, f·v, f²·v, …, fⁿ·v}` carries the natural sl₂ action — h acts
diagonally with weights `n, n−2, …, −n`, e descends one step via the
standard coefficient formula, and f is the raising/lowering nilpotent.
Any non-zero sub-LieSubmodule W of this cyclic space, when one takes the
largest power of f appearing in any element of W and applies `eᵐ`, must
contain a non-zero scalar multiple of v itself; cyclicity then forces W
to be the whole span.

Mathlib provides the underlying `IsSl2Triple` and `HasPrimitiveVectorWith`
machinery from `Mathlib.Algebra.Lie.Sl2` (Oliver Nash 2024): the weight
is automatically a non-negative integer, `fᵏ·v` is non-zero for
`0 ≤ k ≤ n` and zero past `n`, the h-weight of `fᵏ·v` is `n − 2k`, and
the e-action on `fᵏ·v` is given explicitly. The classification result
this theorem states (irreducibility of the cyclic highest-weight module)
does not appear to be exposed by name in Mathlib.

**Scope of irreducibility**: the statement quantifies `W` over
`LieSubmodule R (t.toLieSubalgebra R) M`, i.e. subspaces of `M` closed only
under the **sl₂ subalgebra** generated by `{h, e, f}` — *not* under all
of `L`. This matches Humphreys's setting: classical sl₂ representation
theory describes the structure of M as an sl₂-module, regardless of any
additional Lie algebra actions M may carry. Taking `W` closed under all
of L would be false in general (an earlier draft of this Manifest had
that form; the framework's Backward agent found and documented a
counterexample with `L = sl₂ × ℚ-abelian`).

`(t.toLieSubalgebra R)` is the Mathlib helper that packages the standard
sl₂ triple as a `LieSubalgebra R L`; M inherits the
`LieRingModule (t.toLieSubalgebra R) M` and `LieModule R (t.toLieSubalgebra R) M`
instances by restriction along the inclusion. `LieSubmodule.lieSpan R
(t.toLieSubalgebra R) {v}` is then the cyclic sl₂-LieSubmodule of M
generated by v.
